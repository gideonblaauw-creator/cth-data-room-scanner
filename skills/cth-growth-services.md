# CTH Growth Services — 8-Pillar Due Diligence Framework

Reference skill for scoring startups in CleanTech Hub's Growth Services pipeline.
Read this file before scoring any data room scan.

---

## 1. The 8 Pillars

Each pillar is scored independently on a 1.0–5.0 scale. The overall score is the unweighted average of all 8.

### 1.1 Business Model (bm)

**What to evaluate:**
- Value proposition clarity and differentiation
- Revenue model and pricing strategy
- Unit economics (CAC, LTV, margins)
- Scalability of the model
- Climate/sustainability integration in core business (not bolted on)

**Docs to look for:** One-pager, pitch deck (business model slide), business plan, BMC/Lean Canvas

**Score guide:**
- 5.0: Clear moat, proven unit economics, scalable, climate-native
- 4.0: Solid model, some validation, clear path to scale
- 3.0: Model defined but unproven, unit economics unclear
- 2.0: Vague model, no unit economics, unclear revenue path
- 1.0: No articulated business model

---

### 1.2 Team

**What to evaluate:**
- Founder domain expertise and track record
- Team completeness (tech, commercial, operations)
- Advisory board quality and relevance
- Key hires planned vs. in place
- Founder-market fit for climate/sustainability

**Docs to look for:** CVs/resumes, LinkedIn profiles, team slide in deck, org chart, advisory agreements

**Score guide:**
- 5.0: Serial founders with exits, complete C-suite, top advisors
- 4.0: Strong founders with relevant experience, most key roles filled
- 3.0: Capable founders, some gaps in team, limited advisors
- 2.0: Solo founder or inexperienced team, major gaps
- 1.0: No visible team beyond one founder, no relevant experience

---

### 1.3 Traction

**What to evaluate:**
- Revenue (ARR/MRR) or pre-revenue pipeline
- Number and quality of clients/pilots
- Letters of intent, signed contracts
- Growth rate (MoM or YoY)
- Retention and expansion metrics

**Docs to look for:** Client list, contracts, LOIs, pipeline spreadsheet, revenue dashboard, case studies

**Score guide:**
- 5.0: >$500K ARR, 20%+ MoM growth, strong retention, named clients
- 4.0: $100-500K ARR or 5+ paying clients, clear growth trend
- 3.0: Early revenue or 2-3 pilots, some LOIs
- 2.0: Pre-revenue, 1 pilot or only LOIs, no contracts
- 1.0: No traction, no pipeline, no client conversations

---

### 1.4 Financials

**What to evaluate:**
- Financial projections (3-5 year)
- Current burn rate and runway
- Cap table clarity and cleanliness
- Use of funds breakdown
- Historical financials (if any)
- SAFE/convertible note terms

**Docs to look for:** Financial model (spreadsheet), cap table, SAFE agreements, bank statements, tax filings, use-of-funds slide

**Score guide:**
- 5.0: Detailed model with assumptions, clean cap table, 18+ mo runway, clear use of funds
- 4.0: Reasonable projections, cap table documented, 12+ mo runway
- 3.0: Basic projections, cap table exists but messy, 6-12 mo runway
- 2.0: No financial model, unclear cap table, <6 mo runway
- 1.0: No financials, no cap table, no understanding of burn

---

### 1.5 Impact

**What to evaluate:**
- Climate thesis alignment (direct CO₂ reduction, resource efficiency, circular economy, etc.)
- SDG mapping (which goals, how directly)
- Impact measurement methodology
- ESG/ASG framework adoption
- Additionality — would the impact happen without this company?
- Theory of change articulation

**Docs to look for:** Impact report, ESG assessment, SDG mapping doc, carbon footprint analysis, LCA, certifications (B Corp, etc.)

**Score guide:**
- 5.0: Quantified impact with methodology, third-party validated, core to product
- 4.0: Clear climate thesis, SDG mapping, some measurement in place
- 3.0: Climate-aligned sector, impact stated but not measured
- 2.0: Tangential climate connection, no measurement
- 1.0: No climate/impact narrative, greenwashing risk

---

### 1.6 Legal & IP

**What to evaluate:**
- Corporate structure and jurisdiction
- IP protection (patents, trademarks, trade secrets)
- Regulatory compliance status
- Key contracts and agreements
- Founder agreements and vesting
- Data privacy compliance (if applicable)

**Docs to look for:** Articles of incorporation, patent filings, trademark registrations, founder agreements, shareholder agreements, regulatory permits/licenses, data privacy policy

**Score guide:**
- 5.0: Clean structure, IP protected, all agreements in place, regulatory compliant
- 4.0: Structure clear, some IP protection, most agreements done
- 3.0: Basic structure, IP strategy but not yet filed, some agreements missing
- 2.0: Unclear structure, no IP protection, missing key agreements
- 1.0: No formal structure, no IP strategy, no agreements

---

### 1.7 Market

**What to evaluate:**
- TAM/SAM/SOM sizing with sources
- Competitive landscape analysis
- Market timing and trends
- Go-to-market strategy
- Barriers to entry / defensibility
- Geographic market focus

**Docs to look for:** Market analysis doc, competitive matrix, GTM strategy, industry reports, market slide in deck

**Score guide:**
- 5.0: Data-backed TAM >$1B, detailed competitive analysis, clear GTM, strong timing
- 4.0: Reasonable market sizing, knows competitors, GTM defined
- 3.0: Market size estimated, some competitive awareness, GTM unclear
- 2.0: No market sizing, vague competition awareness
- 1.0: No market analysis, no competitive awareness

---

### 1.8 Investor Readiness

**What to evaluate:**
- Pitch deck quality and narrative
- One-pager availability and quality
- Data room organization and completeness
- Ability to articulate ask, terms, use of funds
- Previous fundraising experience
- Investor pipeline / warm intros available

**Docs to look for:** Pitch deck, one-pager, executive summary, data room index, term sheet (if any), investor CRM/pipeline

**Score guide:**
- 5.0: Polished deck, complete data room, clear ask, investor pipeline warm
- 4.0: Good deck, most docs ready, ask defined, some investor contacts
- 3.0: Basic deck, data room incomplete, ask unclear
- 2.0: Draft deck, minimal data room, no clear ask
- 1.0: No deck, no data room, no fundraising preparation

## 3. Recommendation Thresholds

| Overall Score | Recommendation | Action |
|---------------|----------------|--------|
| ≥ 4.0 | **GO — Phase 1** | Engage immediately. Assign CTH lead, begin Phase 1 acceleration. |
| 3.0 – 3.9 | **Tier 2 — Pipeline** | Strong potential. Monitor, provide targeted support, re-scan in 3-6 months. |
| 2.0 – 2.9 | **Not Ready** | Provide feedback report. Suggest specific improvements. Re-scan when ready. |
| < 2.0 | **Decline** | Does not meet minimum criteria. Provide constructive feedback. |

**Important:** A score of 3.5+ with ≥2 pillars at 4.0+ is also a GO candidate (pattern match: strong in key areas, coachable gaps).

## 4. Eligibility Filters

Before scoring pillars, check these binary/threshold filters. A "fail" on any filter is a blocker regardless of pillar scores.

| Filter | Pass | Warn | Fail |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **Stage** | Pre-seed, Seed, or Series A | Bridge round | Series B+ or idea stage |
| **Round size** | ≥ USD 1,000,000 | USD 500K–1M | < USD 500K |
| **Climate fit** | Core climate/sustainability thesis | Adjacent/indirect climate benefit | No climate connection |
| **Exclusivity** | Open to CTH exclusivity model | Negotiable | Already committed to competing accelerator |
| **Co-creation** | Willing to co-create with CTH | Partially open | Not interested in CTH model |

## 5. Phase 1 Action Plan Template

When recommending GO — Phase 1, include 6-8 concrete actions from this menu:

### Investor Readiness Track
- Redesign pitch deck with CTH narrative framework
- Build/refine one-pager for investor outreach
- Organize and complete data room (checklist provided)
- Prepare financial model with 3-year projections
- Draft SAFE/convertible note terms
- Build target investor list (50+ names with warm intro paths)

### Business Development Track
- Refine value proposition and positioning
- Develop go-to-market playbook
- Identify and warm-intro 3-5 pilot clients
- Structure pilot/POC agreements

### Impact & Compliance Track
- Develop impact measurement framework
- Map to relevant SDGs with quantified targets
- Prepare ESG/ASG baseline assessment
- Review and strengthen IP protection strategy

### Team & Operations Track
- Identify critical hires and draft JDs
- Structure advisory board (2-3 strategic advisors)
- Implement basic financial controls and reporting

---

## 6. Phase 2 Action Plan Template

For Tier 2 pipeline companies or Phase 1 graduates moving to Phase 2:

- Active investor introductions (CTH network)
- Due diligence preparation and practice
- Term sheet negotiation support
- Closing mechanics and legal coordination
- Post-close board setup and governance
