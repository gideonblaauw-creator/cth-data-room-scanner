## Framework Overview (minimal reference until full file arrives)

### The 8 Pillars

1. **Business Model (bm)** — Revenue model, value proposition, unit economics
2. **Team** — Founders, advisors, key hires, track record
3. **Traction** — Clients, pilots, contracts, revenue, growth metrics
4. **Financials** — Projections, burn rate, runway, cap table, use of funds
5. **Impact** — Climate/ESG alignment, SDGs, measurement methodology
6. **Legal & IP** — Corporate structure, IP protection, regulatory compliance
7. **Market** — TAM/SAM/SOM, competition, market dynamics, positioning
8. **Investor Readiness** — Pitch deck, one-pager, data room quality, narrative
